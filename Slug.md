# Slug privacy policy

**In short:** Your saved content stays on your device, unless Android backs it up or you choose
an action that uses another app or service. Slug has no ads or analytics trackers, and the
developer does not receive your personal data.

Last updated: 20 November 2026

Slug is an Android app developed by yhpc.

## Your data

Slug stores your saved content and settings on your device. It does not send your personal
data to the developer, sell your data, or include advertising or analytics trackers.

With your permission, Slug accesses information on your device to provide the functions you
choose. This information is processed on your device. You can change permissions in Android
settings at any time, though some functions may then be unavailable.

## Other apps and backups

Slug only passes information to another app or service when needed to carry out an action you
choose, such as a search or sharing content. That app or service handles the information under
its own privacy policy.

Android may also back up or transfer Slug's local data, depending on your device and backup
settings. Your backup provider's privacy policy applies.

## Deleting your data

You can delete saved content in Slug. Clearing Slug's storage in Android settings or uninstalling
it removes its local data. Anything you choose to save or share through another app is managed
in that app. Backups are managed through your backup provider.

This page will be updated if Slug's privacy practices change. For questions, contact
